STUDY BOT - WHY WE'RE STUCK

We only have: ESP32, small speaker, 2 small LCD screens, ISD1820 voice module.

Problems:

1. No wires, no breadboard, no soldering iron. So we can't connect anything.

2. No proper microphone. The ISD1820's mic is old and low quality, so the bot would mishear us a lot.

3. No amplifier. The speaker would be very quiet and sound robotic.

4. The LCD screens need ~6 wires + a contrast dial each, which we don't have. They may show nothing.

5. Everything pulls power from the tiny USB supply, so it can crash.

6. With the bad mic, the "wake word" won't work well.

Bottom line: the parts we have are the wrong type, and we're missing wires and basic tools. So the device can't be built yet.
